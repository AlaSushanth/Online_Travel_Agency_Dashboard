# Online_Travel_Agency_Dashboard
This dashboard analyzes order and revenue data for an Online Travel Agency, covering flights, hotels, and insurance purchases. It highlights order volume, popular destinations, monthly trends, and key factors driving insurance purchases.

1. Order Types — Breakdown of orders by category: flights, hotels, and insurance.

2. Travel Map Analysis — Popular destinations and most-booked flight types.

3. Insurance Analysis — Distribution of insurance types and what drives customer choice.

4. Monthly Trends — Order volume and revenue across months.

5. Insurance Cost Overview — Cost comparison across insurance types.

6. Key Influencers for Insurance — Top factors influencing insurance purchase decisions.

7. Self-Service Report — Interactive, filterable view for custom exploration.
